Culator Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Overview

Veethreedev created Culator as an offline utility. It is free and intended for use as is.

Information collection and use

Culator does not collect, store, process, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal information. The app does not require internet connectivity and makes no network requests.

Local device storage

Any calculation history or app settings are saved on your device only. This data is never sent to external servers or made accessible to the developer or any third party.

Third-party services

Culator does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or external APIs.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
